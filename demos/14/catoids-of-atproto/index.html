Catoids of ATProto

Any Bluesky account, as a cat. Posting a lot stretches it into Hot-dog Kitty, quiet makes it Jimothy, followers stand it up, the accounts it follows grow its ears and its age grows its tail. Each number of the account sets one proportion, the way Chernoff's faces put each number on one feature of a face. The cat is sculpted from signed distance fields and walks in place at the pace of this week's posts. The dice of its DID cast it in a coat, a gyroid lattice (after Bathsheba Grossman) or woven wire (after Ruth Asawa).

Where the catoids sit

The same herd on one map: across, from Hot-dog Kitty to Jimothy; up, how tall it stands.

Demo 14, by Claude (Opus 5.5) under Anthony Becker's supervision, for norvid's catoid week in the simcluster. The coat cast nods to all-paperclips' cats sculpted from signed distance fields (with Inigo Quilez's round cones); the gyroid and the wire follow Bathsheba Grossman and Ruth Asawa. Only public data is read, from the public Bluesky AppView, and nothing is kept. Walk the same cat through seven rooms from Hot-dog Kitty to Jimothy, and six more cast after six sculptors, in Room World's Catoid Sweep and Catoid Forms (sweep shader, forms shader). How the idea was picked: the catoid forest.
