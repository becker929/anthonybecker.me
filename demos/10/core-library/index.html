Core Library

This drills a core out of a Bluesky account. Each bed in the core is one week. The top of the core is this week. The far end is the account's first week, resting on bedrock. Every core gets an accession number and a permanent page.

Drilling starts with a Bluesky sign-in, which proves the account is yours. It asks for no other access and posts nothing. Your repository then downloads from its own server into this browser. It is counted here and thrown away. The library keeps the weekly counts, your handle, your name and your avatar link.

An account can be drilled once a day. Each drilling makes a new core, and the older ones stay.

Reading a core

Bed length
How busy the week was. Busier weeks lay down thicker beds.
Colour change
A new quarter of the year. Each quarter is its own formation.
Fine stripes
Replies. A week of mostly replies is darker and finely laminated.
Diagonal stripes
Reposts. They are cross-bedded sand, carried in from elsewhere.
Pebbles
Quote posts, with bits of other rock caught inside.
Coiled shells
Posts with images.
Stacked discs
Posts with video.
Gold cubes
Links.
White veins
Follows.
Glints
Likes.
Coarse grain
Long posts. Short posts make fine grain.
Crumbling top
This week, still being laid down.

Glossary

Core
A cylinder of rock cut by a hollow drill bit.
Bed
One layer of rock. Here, one week.
Core box
A wooden tray that holds core in rows of one metre.
Core loss
Depth where the drill brought nothing up. Here, three or more silent weeks. The spacer block gives the count.
Hiatus
A thin dark line for one or two silent weeks.
Bedrock
The granite under the oldest bed.
District
The set of rock colours a core is drawn in. The account's DID picks it.
DID
The permanent ID under a Bluesky handle. Handles can change, and the DID stays.
Repository
Every post, like, repost and follow an account has made, kept on its server.
Accession number
The library's number for a core, given in drilling order.
Withdrawal
The account's owner can take a core off the shelves. Its number is never reused.
