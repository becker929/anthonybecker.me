    Bed length
    How busy the week was. Busier weeks lay down thicker beds.
    Colour change
    A new quarter of the year. Each quarter is its own formation.
    Fine stripes
    Replies. A week of mostly replies is darker and finely laminated.
    Diagonal stripes
    Reposts. They are cross-bedded sand, carried in from elsewhere.
