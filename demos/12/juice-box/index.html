Juice Box

Juice

Tap anything on the shelves. Press the button fast for a combo. The crate takes three hits and the egg three taps. Catch the fireflies after they get out. Open all five boxes for the finale. Each thing closes itself again after a few seconds.

Sound on. Every sound is layered from acoustic and foley samples (knocks, slaps, a box, coins, glass, a cork, celeste and bells) over a sine for the thump, then compressed and given reverb and delay in Ableton Live and rendered. The page only plays the renders.

"Juice" is the game-feel word for everything that makes an action feel good without changing what it does. Nothing on the shelves changes when you switch juice off: the button still counts, the chest still pays out, the crate still breaks. Turn all of it off with Lose it, open something, then switch the effects back on one at a time.
