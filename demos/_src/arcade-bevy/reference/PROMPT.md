# Image prompts

`*_lines.png` and `*_depth.png` are drawn from the model by `blender/lines.py`, from the same
cameras as the game's own captures (`src/shot.rs`). Near is white in the depth pictures.
Give an image model the lines as the second input, after a picture in the style you want.

The prompts used for the look, and what came of them, are in `../LOOK_NOTES.md`.
The textures made from generated pictures are listed in `../assets/ART.md`.
