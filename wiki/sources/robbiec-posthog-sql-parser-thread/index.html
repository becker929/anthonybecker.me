What it is

robbie-c posted the PostHog blog post and replied to many comments. The thread earned 155 points. Some comments are about the website design, which is not covered here. The article itself was not fetched for this record.

What it says

  • Robbie-c says the 70x figure came from a small test corpus. In production, on longer uncached SQL, mean parse time fell 454x.
  • Robbie-c says PostHog SQL started from ClickHouse SQL. The team needed its own parser to add language features.
  • Robbie-c says a proof of concept took about 2 days. A shippable version took about a week.
  • Robbie-c says the old C++ parser will stay as a checking oracle.
  • Cespare argues hand-written parsers are easier to write and maintain than commonly claimed.
  • Keeda says expertise shifts toward building validation around code that an AI wrote.
  • Duendefm worries that heavy AI use will slow the advance of human knowledge. Others disagree.

What evidence it gives

The thread offers the poster's own production figures and answers. The 70x is described as a geometric mean. Other comments are opinions and personal anecdotes. No one in the thread reruns the benchmarks.

Interests and limits

Robbie-c speaks for the team that wrote the parser, and its figures are unchecked. Claims about the linked post are secondhand, because that post was not read. Commenter views on AI are opinions. The thread gives no code.
