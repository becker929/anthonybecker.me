What it is

Quercus Quag Software sells the tool and runs the page. The page promotes the campaign, lists features, and links to the forum, docs and demo.

What it says

  • The page says making games needs no programming or modelling knowledge.
  • It announces a Beyond 1.0 crowdfunding campaign on Indiegogo.
  • It describes a Model Maker that builds tiles, objects and characters from voxels.
  • It lists a map editor, a node-based script editor and a branching dialogue editor.
  • It offers three camera presets, standard, isometric and first-person, plus camera scripting for cutscenes.
  • Games export as standalone builds for Windows and MacOS.

What evidence it gives

The page offers a feature list and a request for support. Its screenshots and community showcase images were not viewed. It gives no independent review or sample project text.

Interests and limits

The seller writes the page and is running a funding campaign. The page gives no figures on sales or users. Nothing was tested. The images were not viewed, so the community showcase is not described. Only the page text was read, and its images were not viewed.
