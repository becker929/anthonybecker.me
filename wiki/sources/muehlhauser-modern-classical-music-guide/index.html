What it is

Luke Muehlhauser wrote this guide and calls the author a fan, not an expert. It aims to help newcomers find modern classical music they might enjoy.

What it says

  • The author defines modern classical as descended from 20th century classical composers, not pop.
  • The post denies the music is unpopular for being harder than much rock.
  • A ten-track quiz asks readers to tell rock from modern classical. The author expects chance results.
  • The author blames thin magazine coverage, costly concerts and few good guides.
  • Film scores are offered as the easiest entry point, since listeners already know their sound.
  • Minimalism, postminimalism and genre-blurring works are also offered as relatively accessible routes.
  • The post calls Boulez, Carter and Stockhausen inaccessible to most listeners. It quotes Babbitt defending that.

What evidence it gives

The post offers personal anecdote, an informal listening game and many linked recordings. It quotes a music history textbook and Milton Babbitt. It gives no survey or data.

Interests and limits

This is one fan's taste and not a study. The explanation for the music's unpopularity is labeled a suspicion. The recommendations were not tested on other listeners, and the guide is undated.
