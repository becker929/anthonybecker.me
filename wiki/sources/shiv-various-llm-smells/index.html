What it is

The author says they used an LLM to polish a math blog late last year. They later saw the same sentence shapes across the internet. The post collects examples under two headings, writing and websites.

What it says

  • The author says LLM-polished drafts felt better, then the same sentence shapes appeared everywhere online.
  • The post lists writing smells. These include punchlines, short-sentence runs and 'not just X, its Y'.
  • The post lists website smells. These include the JetBrains Mono font, step bullets, identical buttons, cards and footnotes.
  • The author says the post is not against AI creative use, only noticing.

What evidence it gives

The evidence is quoted lines from the author's deleted blog and a list of habits.
