What it is

The page introduces a free online book on level design. It lists the sections, recent updates, and the license terms.

What it says

  • The book gathers level design knowledge for 3D video games, for all experience levels.
  • It is split into four books: Process, Culture, Studies and Learning.
  • The Process book opens with a page titled How to make a level.
  • The Culture book lists a history of the level designer, with unfinished pages.
  • The authors warn the book is heavily under construction and some pages are empty.
  • The book is free to read, and the authors say they will never paywall it.

What evidence it gives

The page offers a table of contents and a statement of purpose. It gives no level design advice itself. The chapters linked from it were not read.

Interests and limits
