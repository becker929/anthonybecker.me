What it is

Benjamin Esham writes a personal blog. The post quotes Simon Tatham's article on turning off history. It then offers a function called smite that deletes chosen history entries.

What it says

  • The post quotes Simon Tatham, who disables history and saves useful commands elsewhere.
  • Esham finds the idea uncomfortable and keeps the last 9,800 commands.
  • Esham agrees that recording false starts is useless. Typos and dead ends can trip you up later.
  • The smite zsh function opens an fzf browser of history and deletes chosen entries.
  • The post says multiline commands are not handled correctly. It credits a Stack Overflow answer by Marlon Richert.
  • An update says Eric Nielsen packaged the function into a plugin.

What evidence it gives

The evidence is personal experience and about twenty lines of working zsh code. There are no measurements. The post links a video that was not viewed.

Interests and limits
