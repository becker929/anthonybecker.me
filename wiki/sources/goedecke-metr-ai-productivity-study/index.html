What it is

Sean Goedecke wrote this blog post on 11 July 2025. It reviews METR's study of early-2025 AI and experienced open-source developers. The post explains why the author finds the study strong and what it found. It then offers the author's own explanations for the slowdown.

What it says

  • The post calls the METR study the best AI-in-engineering study the author has seen.
  • Developers expected a 24% speedup and later believed 20%, but were 19% slower.
  • It praises the study for real tasks, expert developers, large codebases and current tools.
  • The author thinks AI helps least on code the developer already knows well.
  • The author suspects the repositories are pure software, which suits AI less.
  • The post says feeling faster may come from lower effort and multitasking during waits.
  • It notes that 69% of developers kept using the AI tooling after the study.

What evidence it gives

The post offers argument and a reading of the METR report. It quotes the study's figures and points to its section on the learning-curve explanation. The author's own explanations rest on personal experience and the types of repositories.

Interests and limits

The post reviews one study and ran no test of its own. Study figures come second hand, and the report was not read for this page. The author labels the explanations as the author's own hypotheses. The author does not claim the result holds for other developers or tasks.
