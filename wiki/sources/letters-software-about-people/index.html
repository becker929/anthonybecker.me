Software is about people, not code

A short letter telling new developers that software exists for people, not for its code.

What it is

Dan, who publishes as mooreds, writes advice letters for new developers. This letter describes the author's early focus on code. It says people matter more for a project's success.

What it says

  • The author says abandoned codebases showed that people matter more than code for success.
  • The letter says a well-built codebase that does the wrong thing is useless.
  • The letter says code must solve real-world problems of real people.

What evidence it gives

The letter gives one personal anecdote: some codebases abandoned for lack of a market. It offers no data and no detailed examples.

Interests and limits

The letter rests on one person's experience. The site also promotes a book and a newsletter by the author. The letter takes about a minute to read.
