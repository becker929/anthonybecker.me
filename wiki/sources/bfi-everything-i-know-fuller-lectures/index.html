What it is

The Buckminster Fuller Institute published the page. It introduces a transcript of lectures Fuller gave in January 1975. The page links twelve sections of the transcript and mentions a video series on archive.org.

What it says

  • Buckminster Fuller gave a lecture series on all of Fuller's work in January 1975.
  • The lectures span 42 hours. They cover the Dymaxion house, car and bathroom, domes, tensegrity and Synergetics.
  • The institute says the lectures are autobiographical in parts. They place Fuller's history within the history of science and industrialization.
  • The transcript was typed word for word from audiotapes and is minimally edited. It keeps Fuller's own phrases and sentence structures.
  • The institute invites readers to send marked-up edits and annotations. The page thanks a volunteer transcriber and an outline by Ed Applewhite.

What evidence it gives

The page offers description only. It gives the length, topics, transcription method and credits. The lecture text itself sits in twelve linked sections, which were not opened.

Interests and limits

Only the introduction and section previews were read; the 12 transcripts were not opened. The institute carries Fuller's name and works to promote Fuller's ideas. Its description of the lectures as extraordinary is its own claim. Nothing here tests Fuller's claims.
