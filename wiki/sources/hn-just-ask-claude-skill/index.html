What it is

Posted by surprisetalk, the thread links to an essay by Justis Mills. The essay gives several reasons a friend may not just ask an AI. This page covers the essay as read and the thread's replies.

What it says

  • The essay says people asking for help usually could not ask their own AI.
  • The essay says knowing what to ask for matters, even when AI does the work.
  • The essay adds that a friend may care and want an excuse to talk.
  • Commenter pavel_lishin says you can simply answer that you do not know.
  • Commenter adamddev1 warns that non-experts cannot tell when an AI answer is false.
  • Commenters compare it to googling for others, citing the LMGTFY site.
  • Much of the thread debates YouTube Premium against ad blockers.

What evidence it gives

The essay is a short humorous opinion piece with no data. The thread adds anecdotes from commenters. No one presents measurements.

Interests and limits

The essay is humorous and states a personal feeling, not a finding. Only two of the Substack page's comments were shown, so the rest were not read. The thread is made of self-selected commenters. Large parts of it are off topic, about YouTube ads.
