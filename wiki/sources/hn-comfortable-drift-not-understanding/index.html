What it is

This is a discussion of an ergosphere.blog post. The post imagines Alice and Bob producing equal papers while only Alice learns. Commenters, many of them software developers, debate whether that matters.

What it says

  • Commenter stavros argues Bob got the same useful result as Alice with an agent.
  • Several replies say the program exists to produce scientists, not results.
  • Several commenters reject the calculator analogy because students learn arithmetic by hand first.
  • Commenter sd9 expects the market to stop valuing the skills Bob skips.
  • Commenter klabb3 warns the AI gives plausible answers Bob cannot judge as right or wrong.
  • Commenter mtrifonov says the drift comes from the agent setup, not from AI itself.

What evidence it gives

The thread offers opinion, analogy and personal anecdote. Commenters mention calculators, cooking, assembly language and their own jobs. A few cite figures such as AI company revenue or model costs without proof. No study is presented.

Interests and limits

Commenters are self-selected, and many are software developers, so the debate drifts toward programming. Claims about costs, model progress and jobs are asserted, not checked. The post under discussion is not the source here. Only the first part of the thread was read, so later replies are not covered.
