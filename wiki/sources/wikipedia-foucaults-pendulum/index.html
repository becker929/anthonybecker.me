What it is

This is a Wikipedia entry on the novel Foucault's Pendulum. It gives publication facts, the plot and themes. It also lists occult groups in the book. It compares the book with other works.

What it says

  • The article says the 1988 novel is divided into ten Sefiroth segments.
  • It says three editors invent a conspiracy called The Plan.
  • It says a note behind The Plan proves to be a merchant's laundry list.
  • It reads the book as a satire of conspiracy theories, not an endorsement of them.
  • It says Eco rejected any intended reference to Michel Foucault in the title.
  • It says Eco called Dan Brown a character in the novel.

What evidence it gives

The article cites critics such as Burgess and Hutcheon, and interviews with Eco. It quotes a few passages from the novel. The plot summary carries few citations.
