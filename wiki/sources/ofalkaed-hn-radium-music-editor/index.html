What it is

The poster shared a link to the Radium page, and commenters took it from there. The page itself was not read. The thread is the source described here.

What it says

  • Commenter bane says trackers allow fast, typed note entry.
  • Commenter drannex argues that trackers and FL Studio group music by loops, unlike other DAWs.
  • Commenters quote the Radium page saying its source is open.
  • Users report that Radium is stable on Linux but less so on Windows.
  • One commenter reports slow, unstable use on Windows, and old OctaMED files failing.
  • Commenters name other trackers and DAWs as alternatives, with Renoise called the most popular.

What evidence it gives

The thread offers user anecdotes and opinion, with no benchmarks. One long reply compares tracker and lane-based workflows from the commenter's own experience. Some replies link a manual and a video tutorial.

Interests and limits

No Radium developer was identified in the thread. The Radium page, manual and linked videos were not opened. Stability reports are few and differ by platform.
