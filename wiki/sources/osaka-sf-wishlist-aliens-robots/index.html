What it is

Osaka posts two short items about taste in science fiction. Two other users reply with titles that might fit.

What it says

  • The author wants science fiction with aliens, giant robots and a hopeful view of humanity.
  • A cool girl may replace the alien or the robot, never both.
  • A follow-up ranks stories as excellent, phenomenal or perfect, with perfect adding a kiss.
  • The follow-up says the cool figure must always be fighting evil.
  • The author calls Macross Frontier fine, but dislikes its messy storytelling.

What evidence it gives

None beyond opinion. The replies name two titles, and the author gives reasons for disliking one.

Interests and limits
