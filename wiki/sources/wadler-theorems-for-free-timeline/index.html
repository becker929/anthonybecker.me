What it is

Type Classes, a training site for Haskell, hosts the entry. It marks the paper as the origin of what are now called free theorems. The page credits Philip Wadler as author.

What it says

  • The paper introduced what are now called free theorems.
  • Given only the type of a polymorphic function, a theorem it satisfies can be stated.
  • The paper's stated purpose is to explain the trick behind this.

What evidence it gives

The page offers only a quoted passage from the paper and a date. It gives no proof, example or code. The trick itself is not described on the page.

Interests and limits

Only the short timeline entry was read, not Wadler's paper. The host site sells Haskell courses and projects. The page says nothing about how far free theorems reach.
