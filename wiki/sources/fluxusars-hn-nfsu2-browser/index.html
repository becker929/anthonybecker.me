What it is

The thread starts from a link to a browser version of the game. Commenters describe free roam and online races with friends. Commenters add experience reports and argue about the law.

What it says

  • Fluxusars posted a link to a browser version of the game with online play.
  • Many report smooth play, even on phones, but some saw poor speed.
  • A commenter quotes a README saying the x86 code is translated to C, then WebAssembly.
  • Many commenters doubt the legality of hosting the game and taking donations.
  • Others note the publisher no longer sells it, and licensed music complicates a re-release.
  • Some commenters say the port was built with AI coding tools.

What evidence it gives

The thread offers first-hand play reports, links to a code repository, and opinion on copyright. Several legal claims come from commenters who say they are not lawyers. No claim was checked.

Interests and limits

Comments are individual opinions and anecdotes, not measurements. The legal arguments are not authoritative. The thread says little about how the online servers work. The linked site was only glanced at and not used.
