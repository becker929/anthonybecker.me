What it is

What it says

  • Guy says people feel trapped between luxury and fast fashion, though mid-tier clothing still exists.
  • Guy says online shopping multiplied options, which paradoxically made building a wardrobe harder.
  • Guy says the article covers four companies trying to keep customers engaged.
  • One reply calls $190 chinos and $150 shirts nonstarters. Another says $150 for a decent shirt is not outrageous.
  • Replies raise sizing limits, with one noting the average US male waist is 40.
  • Many commenters say they thrift, and others ask for women's or European equivalents.
