What it is

Phil Bagwell wrote this report, which is listed in the EPFL Infoscience catalog. It proposes the VList as a replacement for the linked list in functional programming. The catalog page gives only the abstract.

What it says

  • The report introduces the VList, which the abstract calls compact, thread safe and faster.
  • The abstract claims space use falls by 50% to 90%. Typical list operations get faster by factors of 4 to 20 or more.
  • Indexing and length drop from O(N) to O(1) and O(lgN) respectively.
  • The abstract says a Common Lisp dialect interpreter, Visp, was built on VLists. It was compared with OCAML.
  • The structure also adapts to variable length arrays, persistent deques and functional hash tables.

What evidence it gives

The abstract mentions a benchmark comparison against OCAML. The abstract gives no details of the tests. Nothing beyond the abstract was read.
