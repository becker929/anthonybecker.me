What it is

troupo asks for examples of dense user interfaces, after finding little from search engines. Commenters answer with sites, apps, terminals, cockpits and games. The thread is not about one article. The post cites a blog by Matthew Ström.

What it says

  • The poster, troupo, says finding dense UI examples seemed impossible. Search turned up one blog post.
  • Commenters name the Bloomberg Terminal as the obvious example of a dense, expert interface.
  • Audio and video tools are named often. Ableton Live is praised but has a steep learning curve.
  • Parts-catalog sites such as McMaster-Carr and RockAuto are praised. A commenter quotes a former McMaster developer crediting Tufte.
  • Some commenters argue dense interfaces suit experts who invest time in learning them.
  • One commenter blames whitespace-heavy design on Tailwind UI and advice from Refactoring UI.
  • Several commenters share their own dense projects. One is a Hacker News front page.

What evidence it gives

The thread offers opinion, anecdote and links. Some commenters worked on products they name. Examples are a Wunderground forecast view and a JPL rover tool. Nobody in the thread offers measurements of density.

Interests and limits

Commenters promote their own projects, and the post carries no check on that. Linked sites and screenshots were not opened. Replies disagree, for example on whether Blender or Bloomberg is usable. The thread shows what readers of one forum favor.
