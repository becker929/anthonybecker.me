What it is

The page is a calendar kept by the 19hz site. It has no named author. Entries range from free bar nights to large ticketed shows. Filters cover San Francisco, Oakland, San Jose and Sacramento.

What it says

  • The page lists upcoming electronic music events in the Bay Area and Northern California.
  • Each entry gives date, title, venue, genre tags, price, age limit and organizers.
  • The page says most updates happen on Wednesdays or Thursdays.

What evidence it gives

The page offers listings only: no reviews, no commentary and no ranking. Tags include house, techno, drum and bass, jungle, synthpop and many others. A corrections email is posted for readers.

Interests and limits

This is a snapshot that changes as events are added or pass. The page does not say how events are chosen, so coverage may be uneven. Events from late November 2026 to early February 2027 were not read. Most of the venue and promoter lists were not read either. The page has no argument to assess.
