What it is

Barnacle Press hosts a comic supplement of early strips. This page introduces The Outbursts of Everett True by A.D. Condo. It lists strips by date.

What it says

  • The page says each strip has Everett suffer an outrage, then beat someone up.
  • The page says Everett follows a firm ethos and thrashes cops and preachers too.

What evidence it gives

The page offers the editor's description only. The strip images were not viewed, so nothing here rests on the artwork.

Interests and limits

The introduction is the editor's opinion, with no outside sources. Only the page text was read, and the strip images were not viewed. The date list in the text starts in July 1905.
