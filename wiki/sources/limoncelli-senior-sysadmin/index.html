What makes a sysadmin a "senior sysadmin"?

What it is

Tom Limoncelli wrote the post for the blog on system administration. It came out of a recent discussion of junior and senior staff. The post sets out the author's own test.

What it says

  • Limoncelli says a senior sysadmin knows the systems' internals and debugs by science, not guessing.
  • A senior sysadmin recalls past fixes but still checks that the symptoms match.
  • The post says a senior sysadmin automates away problems instead of working harder.
  • Limoncelli ranks technical leadership highest: writing processes so that others can follow them.
  • The post says good documentation is the first step from guesswork to repeatable to automated.
  • It contrasts the old way of hoarding information with the new way of sharing it.

What evidence it gives

The post offers argument and two examples: a disk-replacement procedure and a monitoring system. It gives no data or study. Readers add their own views in the comments.

Interests and limits

This is a personal view from 2012, and it covers system administration only. Limoncelli writes books on the topic, and the blog promotes them. One commenter warns that sharing knowledge fails if managers do not reward it.
