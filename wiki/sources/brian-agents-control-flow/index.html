What it is

The author signs as brian and writes a short thesis on reliable agents. The post sets out to say why prompts alone cannot carry complex tasks.

What it says

  • The post argues reliable agents need deterministic control flow in code. Longer prompt chains are not enough.
  • The author says MANDATORY or DO NOT SKIP show prompting has hit its ceiling.
  • The post compares prompt chains to a language where statements are mere suggestions.
  • It proposes scaffolds with explicit state transitions and validation checkpoints. The model is one component.
  • It says an agent without error detection quickly reaches wrong conclusions.
  • Without programmatic verification, the post says, the options are babysitter, auditor or prayer.

What evidence it gives

The post offers argument only. It has no code, benchmarks or worked examples. The page is very short.

Interests and limits

The post is one person's opinion and gives no test of its claim. It does not say how much control flow is enough. It does not name any product or employer with a stake in the view.
