What it is

A brief reference article on John Murray Spear, who lived from 1804 to 1887. It has sections on early life, the machine, and later life.

What it says

  • Spear was a Universalist minister and abolitionist. Spear also worked on prison reform.
  • In 1852 Spear left the Universalists for Spiritualism. Spear claimed contact with the Association of Electrizers.
  • The article says evidence indicates Spear occasionally faked signatures from these spirit guides.
  • Spear and followers built the New Motive Power in Lynn, Massachusetts. It was a messianic perpetual motion machine.
  • A ritual birth of the machine failed, and hostile neighbors later destroyed it.

What evidence it gives

The article cites a 2006 biography by John Benedict Buescher. It also cites a finding aid and a book on Spiritualism. It reproduces Spear's business card text. Claims are mostly single-sentence statements.
