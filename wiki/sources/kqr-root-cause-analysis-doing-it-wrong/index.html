What it is

kqr writes the Entropic Thoughts blog. The draft covers basic concepts, difficulties such as hindsight bias, and four CAST steps. A note at the top says the topic was split into separate, better articles.

What it says

  • kqr says root cause analysis rests on simplified models that give poor understanding.
  • The draft recommends CAST, drawing mostly on Leveson's CAST Handbook.
  • An accident needs a system in a hazardous state meeting unfavourable conditions, per the draft.
  • kqr says human error is where an investigation starts, not where it ends.
  • In the example, a support report named one settings change as root cause and stopped.
  • The draft says phrases like "the operator must" usually signal hindsight bias.
  • It lays out four steps, from describing the system to finding unsafe interactions.

What evidence it gives

The draft offers argument and one outage from the author's own work. Names are replaced. It draws on the CAST Handbook and gives no data or comparison of methods. Its example analysis lists questions and expected safety controls.

Interests and limits

The author calls the page mostly unedited stream-of-consciousness and says the later articles are better. In the method section, step 3 is empty and step 4 holds two note fragments. The page carries unfinished notes. The author says they are no CAST expert. The handbook and the linked articles were not read.
