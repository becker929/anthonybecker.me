What it is

Terence Tao is a mathematician who writes the What's new blog. The post asks whether dimensional analysis has a properly grounded mathematical foundation. It is tagged expository and uses group and vector space language.

What it says

  • The post says dimensional consistency helps catch errors and reveals the form of laws.
  • It models quantities as depending on unit parameters, as representations of a group.
  • It says a dimensionally consistent identity need only be checked for one choice of units.
  • It says varying units and using inconsistent equations cannot both be kept.
  • It says dimensional analysis gives necessary conditions on Sobolev inequality exponents.
  • It says position and displacement vectors transform differently.
  • In the abstract model, quantities live in one-dimensional vector spaces.

What evidence it gives

The post offers definitions and worked examples. It shows how the Sobolev inequality constrains its exponents. It gives no experiments or data. Claims rest on mathematical argument, and some facts are referred to other notes.

Interests and limits

The post is an exposition, not a new theorem. The equations did not come through in the downloaded text. The reach is mathematical modelling, not a physics test.
