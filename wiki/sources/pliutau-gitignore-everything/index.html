What it is

Alex Pliutau writes the packagemain newsletter. The free preview sets up an alternative to the usual .gitignore.

What it says

  • Pliutau says developers often commit junk such as .DS_Store, node_modules, IDE settings or CLAUDE.md.
  • The post proposes ignoring everything by default and allowing only specific files.

What evidence it gives

The preview gives no examples, code or data. It offers only a short argument from common experience.

Interests and limits

Only the free preview, about two paragraphs, was readable; the rest is behind a paywall. The proposed file rules are not in the preview, so the method is unknown. The page also promotes a paid subscription.
