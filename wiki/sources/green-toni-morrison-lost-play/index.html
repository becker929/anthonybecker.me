What it is

Elon Green wrote this article for Vulture, part of New York magazine. It follows the play from its start to opening night. It also corrects a rumor that all copies were destroyed.

What it says

  • Dreaming Emmett ran for four weeks in Albany in 1986 and was never fully restaged.
  • A rumor said Morrison destroyed all copies, but the article says copies survive.
  • Morrison first imagined Emmett Till returning from the dead to take retribution.
  • Revisions turned the lead into a Chicago teenager shot over a kite in 1957.
  • The budget passed $70,000, against an average of $27,686 for that season.
  • A Capital Rep historian says the play grossed over $85,000 despite mixed reviews.
  • The article suggests the play helped Morrison think through the ghost in Beloved.

What evidence it gives

The article cites archives at Princeton and Columbia and an internal budget memo. It quotes producers, a designer, cast members and reviews. It reports Namwali Serpell's reading of an early Beloved outline.

Interests and limits

The author has a forthcoming book on Michael Stewart, whose death inspired the play. The Beloved link rests on a Morrison letter and Serpell's reading. The article hedges it. The article passes its own judgments on the play. Some sources are recollections from decades later.
