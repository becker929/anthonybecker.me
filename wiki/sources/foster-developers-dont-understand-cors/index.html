What it is

Chris Foster writes about a 2019 Zoom vulnerability found by Jonathan Leitschuh. The post argues that the flaw came from a misunderstanding of CORS.

What it says

  • Foster says too many web developers do not understand how CORS works.
  • Foster relays a report that Zoom ran a web server on localhost port 19421.
  • Foster says the claim that browsers ignore CORS for localhost is incorrect.
  • Foster says Zoom's image workaround let every website trigger the app, not only Zoom.
  • Foster proposes setting Access-Control-Allow-Origin to https://zoom.us and blocking iframes.
  • Foster objects that a link click should not expose the camera and microphone.
  • Foster asks whether CORS is too complex or developers need better education.

What evidence it gives

One case study, drawn from another researcher's report. The post adds anecdotes from consulting work and mentions Stack Overflow answers. It gives no counts or tests.

Interests and limits

The post speaks from a consultant's experience and gives no survey of developers. It says it cannot know whether CORS confusion caused the Zoom design. It relays a Reddit comment about Firefox and rebuts it without a test. The post is from 2019.
