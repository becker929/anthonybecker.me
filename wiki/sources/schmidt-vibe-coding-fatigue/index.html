What it is

Stephan Schmidt writes about building Marvai with Claude Code and Cursor. The post describes a fatigue the author had not felt before. It offers causes and a short list of needs.

What it says

  • The author feels fatigued after as little as one hour of reviewing fast AI output.
  • AI output arrives faster than the brain can form a coherent picture, the author says.
  • The post links the fatigue to a faster dopamine loop and more context switches.
  • Reviewing and directing AI output is more stressful than writing code, the author says.
  • The author compares it to a factory machine setting the pace of a worker.
  • The post proposes deliberate pacing, AI-aware retrospectives and attention to mental health.

What evidence it gives

The evidence is one person's experience on a personal project, plus a factory analogy. The post cites the book Team Topologies for cognitive load. No survey, measurement or study is given. One commenter says the post matches their own feelings.

Interests and limits

The author builds Marvai, a prompt package manager, alone. Fatigue is self-reported and untested. Causes such as dopamine are asserted without citation. The post dates from July 2025.
