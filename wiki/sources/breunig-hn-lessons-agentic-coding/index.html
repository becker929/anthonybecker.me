What it is

The linked post is on dbreunig.com and dated 4 May 2026. It lists ten lessons for coding with agents. It asks what to do when code is cheap. The thread gathers reactions from many commenters.

What it says

  • The linked post lists ten lessons, including implement to learn and document intent.
  • The post says code is cheap but maintenance, support and security are not.
  • Commenter boesboes warns costs may rise 10 to 100 times later.
  • Commenter spicyusername reports shipping four features at a time across several projects.
  • Commenters such as yodsanklai describe agent code that missed a one line fix.
  • Commenter HarHarVeryFunny says coding is a small part of the job, limiting gains.
  • Commenter asG1298 says the author works for the Overture Map Foundation, which has big sponsors.

What evidence it gives

The post is a list of tips with no data. The thread is anecdote and argument. One commenter cites defect rates and shipping frequency without a source. Another reports a 30 second stress test falling to about 8ms.

Interests and limits

Commenters are self-selected and many are professional programmers. Claims of large speedups or failures are first-hand and unmeasured. The comment about the author's employer was not checked. Predictions about prices and jobs are guesses.
