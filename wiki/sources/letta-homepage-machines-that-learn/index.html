What it is

Letta describes itself as an AI research lab in San Francisco. The page lists research posts, a product, press items, team background and backers.

What it says

  • Letta says it builds machines that learn, with agents that remember and improve over time.
  • The page lists six research posts, from October 2023 to October 2026.
  • It presents Letta Agent as the shipped product of its research, installed through npm.
  • It says the founders created MemGPT at UC Berkeley and names its backers.

What evidence it gives

The page offers no data. It gives titles and one-line summaries of research posts that were not opened. Press items are listed by outlet and headline only.

Interests and limits

The page is the company's own pitch, and the company sells the product it describes. None of the linked research or press was read. Claims about agents that learn are stated as goals.
