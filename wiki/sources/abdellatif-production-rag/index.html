What it is

The author describes eight months of work on retrieval-augmented generation. The two projects held 9M and 4M pages. The post lists what helped and what the stack ended up being.

What it says

  • The author's team built RAG for two projects, with 9M and 4M pages.
  • A prototype that worked on 100 documents gave subpar results at production scale.
  • The author ranks five fixes by return, starting with query generation and reranking.
  • The best reranker setup was 50 chunks in and 15 out.
  • The vector database moved from Azure to Pinecone to Turbopuffer.

The post is the author's account of two projects. It gives no benchmarks, figures or code beyond a stack list.
