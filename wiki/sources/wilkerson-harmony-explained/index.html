What it is

Daniel Shawcross Wilkerson's preprint argues that music theory needs a real scientific basis. It considers physical sound together with the computation any listening machine must perform. This page rests on the abstract, because the arXiv page offered no PDF.

What it says

  • The paper claims to derive the major scale, the standard chord dictionary and major-minor feeling.
  • It credits Helmholtz and Birkhoff with earlier derivations of the major scale.
  • It calls the chord and triad derivations original.
  • It says Helmholtz's concord theory is incomplete, because more notes could only penalize a sound.

What evidence it gives

The abstract states claims and offers derivations, without experimental results. It is a preprint, and this page has not assessed its argument directly.

Interests and limits

It compares most music theory books to medieval medical textbooks, so its tone is combative. See the Hacker News discussion for pushback. Its claims concern tonal music, which is a limited scope.
