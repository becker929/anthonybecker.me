What it is

This is the English Wikipedia entry for a novel about rival posthuman factions. Volunteer editors write it, and no author is named. It aims to inform readers about the book and its universe.

What it says

  • Schismatrix was nominated for the 1985 Nebula Award and a 1986 British award.
  • Shapers change bodies by genetics, while Mechanists use software and external changes.
  • The plot follows Abelard Lindsay across decades of factional war and political scheming.
  • The alien Investors arrive and bring a period of trade and peace.
  • The article lists ideologies including Preservationism, Detente, Cataclysm and Post-Humanism.
  • A 1986 review by Dave Langford praised the novel as exciting and full of ideas.
  • Two companion stories, Swarm and Spider Rose, were adapted for Love, Death & Robots.

What evidence it gives

The article relies on a short reference list, mostly award pages and magazine reviews. The plot, character and setting sections are uncited summary. Four reviews are quoted from 1986 to 1991 magazines.
