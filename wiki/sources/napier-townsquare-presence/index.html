What it is

The page is by Cauê Napier. It presents TownSquare as a small presence layer. Visitors to a site can see each other, chat briefly and move around.

What it says

  • The page says one script tag gives a site a shared space where visitors chat.
  • The page says it needs no accounts, no algorithms and no build step.
  • The page says each site gets its own square. Sites can join a public map.

What evidence it gives

The page offers a description, a live demo and three short visitor quotes. It gives no technical detail beyond the script tag.

Interests and limits

The live demo, the map and the linked blog and docs were not viewed. The page is short. The visitor quotes are unattributed. The page says adding TownSquare is free and takes about a minute. The author has an interest as maker of the tool.
