What it is

The page links to emulators of machines such as the ZX Spectrum and Commodore C64. Each entry offers a program to start.

What it says

  • The page lists emulated machines including the KC85, Amstrad CPC, ZX Spectrum and Commodore VIC-20.
  • The page lists many demos and games, with credits to groups such as Oxyron.
  • Some entries carry hints, such as selecting the Kempston joystick or pressing SPACE to start.

What evidence it gives

The page offers only a list of names and short hints. It has no description or claims.

Interests and limits

Only the page text was read, and the emulators were not run. The page names no author, and the contents of each program were not checked.
