What it is

The page is the public front door for the Bevy engine. It sets out to sell developers on its design and to attract donations.

What it says

  • The page calls Bevy a data-driven game engine built in Rust.
  • It lists 2D and 3D renderers that both allow custom shaders, materials and render pipelines.
  • It lists support for Windows, MacOS, Linux, Web, iOS and Android.
  • It claims hot reloading of scenes, textures and meshes without restarting a running app.
  • It claims compile times of 0.8 to 3.0 seconds with the fast compiles configuration.
  • It says the engine is free forever, under MIT or Apache 2.0 licenses.

What evidence it gives

The page offers feature lists and one compile-time claim. It gives no benchmark, test setup or example game. A compile-time comparison is made with unnamed engines.

Interests and limits

The Bevy project writes the page and asks for donations. The compile-time comparison names no rival and shows no method. Nothing was tested. The page says nothing about maturity or stability.
