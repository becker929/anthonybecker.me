What it is

The Granule project is an academic research effort on type systems. The page gives a short mission statement. It then lists news from March 2020 to August 2026.

What it says

  • The project wants types to capture how a program computes, not just what.
  • Its main mechanism is graded types combined with linear and dependent types.
  • Graded modal types with linear types aim to enforce resource use in the language.
  • The group is developing the Granule language, plus a dependent type theory implemented in Gerty.
  • An August 2026 ICFP paper, Same Coeffect, Different Base, connects two approaches to graded types.
  • A 2022 ECOOP paper on inverting a type won two distinguished awards.
  • A November 2024 grant from ARIA funds graded modal types for probabilistic programming.
