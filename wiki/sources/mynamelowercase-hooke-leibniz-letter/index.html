What it is

The author of the blog Drawn In Perspective found the letter in Royal Society archives. The post opens a planned series on 17th century science and cyberpunk. It explains the letter's context and offers a reading of it.

What it says

  • The post says Hooke wrote to Leibniz in 1681, praising a universal science language.
  • It says Leibniz's Characteristica Universalis aimed to automate scientific reasoning.
  • The author says the transcription of the letter is about 90% accurate so far.
  • The post claims Hooke hoped such a language would protect individuals from censoring authorities.
  • It argues this hope makes the letter cyberpunk, not only cybernetic.
  • The author proposes Hooke as patron saint of cyberpunk, echoing Wiener on Leibniz.
  • A reader posted a full transcription, which the author later linked.

What evidence it gives

The evidence is one archival letter, shown as a scan and quoted in part. The author's transcription is unfinished. The reading leans on a remark by Norbert Wiener. No other Hooke writing is analysed here.

Interests and limits

This is a personal blog post, and the author calls the cyberpunk link anachronistic. The author hedges with "If my reading of this letter is accurate". Hooke's wider views are left for later posts. The letter scan and the linked transcriptions were not opened. The post and its comments were read in full.
