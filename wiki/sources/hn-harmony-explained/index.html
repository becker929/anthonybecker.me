What it is

This thread discusses Wilkerson's paper and drew 105 points and 114 comments. Musicians and engineers argue about what a scientific theory of harmony can explain. Wilkerson joins to defend it as computer science.

What it says

  • klik99 calls claims about pleasant ratios and missing-feeling minor chords simply wrong.
  • TheOtherHobbes says consonance depends partly on frequency, and points to Sethares and Tymoczko.
  • TheOtherHobbes calls the idea that major is happy and minor sad a simplification.
  • atoav finds the most consonant combinations boring and other aspects of music more interesting.
  • MetaWhirledPeas says a perfect scientific theory would take the fun out of music.
  • Wilkerson, dswilkerson, asks commenters to read the paper before responding.

What evidence it gives

The comments give personal experience and pointers to other work. None of those listed reports a test of the paper.

Interests and limits

A thread shows who chose to reply. Commenters are pseudonymous, and their expertise cannot be checked from the thread. The paper's author has an obvious stake.
