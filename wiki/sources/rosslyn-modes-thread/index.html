What it is

Rosslynratioed wrote the thread in reply to freya.bsky.social. An earlier post in the chain asks for help from this thread's author.

What it says

  • The thread defines modes as permutations of scales, treated as cyclic subgroups under Z12.
  • It says each mode arises from the chord harmony of its parent scale.
  • It argues the usual lesson, D Dorian as C major from D, misses the point. It says the harmonic shift makes modes distinct.
  • It advises hearing modes over chords, vamps or drones.
  • It suggests playing C major over a D drone or D minor 7 vamp.

What evidence it gives

The thread offers argument and one listening exercise. It gives no recordings, sources or notation.

Interests and limits
