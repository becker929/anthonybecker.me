What it is

Wikipedia's editors describe Ky, born in 1996 in Abidjan. The article covers how the hair sculptures began and how the work became political. It also lists a fashion brand, shows and screen roles.

What it says

  • The article says Ky sculpts wire, thread and extended dreadlocks while they stay attached.
  • It says a 2016 photo album of pre-colonial African women inspired Ky to start.
  • It says each sculpture takes between twenty minutes and six hours.
  • It says Ky's work grew political, covering bullying, breast flattening, genital mutilation and abortion law.
  • It says Ky was in the 2022 Venice Biennale and has a fashion brand, Kystroy.
  • It says a 2023 Télérama interview omitted Ky's stance against transgender rights. Public outcry led to a correction.

What evidence it gives

The article cites press pieces and archived pages for each claim. It includes quotes attributed to Ky from interviews. It has no images described in the text read.
