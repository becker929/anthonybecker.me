What it is

Scott Spence writes a personal blog. This post describes speeding up a zsh shell that took over five seconds to open. The page says it was updated after the original date.

What it says

  • Spence says zsh took more than five seconds to open a new terminal.
  • The post says zsh's built-in zprof tool profiles startup.
  • The profile blamed Oh-My-Zsh for over half of startup time.
  • Spence rebuilds the completion cache only once a day instead of every startup.
  • The post reports startup fell from about 5s to about 0.5s, a tenfold gain.
  • Spence advises trying fixes one at a time and keeping a fast-enough setup.

What evidence it gives

The post gives profiler percentages, config snippets, and full before and after files. A table lists approximate totals. It does not show how the totals were timed.

Interests and limits

It reflects one machine and one setup with Oh-My-Zsh and the Spaceship prompt. The totals are approximate. Other setups may gain less. The linked posts and sources were not read.
