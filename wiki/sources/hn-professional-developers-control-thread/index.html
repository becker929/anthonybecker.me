What it is

Posted by dpflan, the thread links to arXiv paper 2512.14012. Commenters quote its field study of 13 developers and survey of 99. The paper concerns how professionals work with AI coding agents. This page describes the thread, not the paper.

What it says

  • Commenter game_the0ry says 13 observed and 99 surveyed developers is too few.
  • Commenter flurie replies that qualitative research is judged by thematic saturation, not significance.
  • Commenter simonw says the study used mostly GPT-5 and Claude Sonnet 4. It was too early to catch 5.x Codex or Claude 4.5 models.
  • Several commenters say supervising agents leaves them more mentally tired than coding.
  • Commenters split on whether agents work on large professional codebases.
  • A long sub-thread argues about whether enjoying programming should shape career choices.

What evidence it gives

The thread is opinion and personal anecdote from self-selected commenters. A few comments report details of the paper, such as dates and sample sizes. One comment quotes the paper's definition of agentic tools. None of the commenters present measurements.

Interests and limits

The paper itself was not read for this page. Descriptions of it come from commenters and may be wrong. Commenters are self-selected, and many hold strong views on AI and jobs. Much of the thread drifts away from the paper. The paper's own conclusions are only partly quoted in the thread.
