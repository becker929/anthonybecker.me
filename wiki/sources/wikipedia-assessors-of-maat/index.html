What it is

This is a Wikipedia entry on the Assessors of Maat. It explains their role in the afterlife judgment. It lists each judge with its origin and its sin.

What it says

  • The article says 42 minor deities joined Osiris in judging the dead.
  • It says Book of the Dead chapter 125 names each judge and a matching declaration.
  • It says the heart was weighed against a feather. A heavier heart was eaten by Ammit.
  • It says a balanced heart made the dead person Maa Kheru, meaning vindicated.
  • It notes that some read the 42 declarations as precedents for the Ten Commandments.
  • It says the declarations look like divine versions of daily morality, not revealed norms.
  • It says Wilkinson's list of tasks differs from translations of the Papyrus of Ani.

What evidence it gives

The article cites books by Hart, Wilkinson, Budge, Taylor and Faulkner. It gives a table of 42 judges. The table lists names, places, sins and Papyrus of Ani lines. It offers no new research.
