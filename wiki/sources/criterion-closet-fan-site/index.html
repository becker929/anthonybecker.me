What it is

The page is a web app made by Oliver and Patrick Evans. It lets visitors browse Criterion films by spine number, title or year. It credits Wikidata and the Criterion shop for its data.

What it says

  • The page is a fan-made browser for 1,327 films in the Criterion Collection.
  • It offers list and closet views, filters, a random pick and a four-film picks card.
  • The page says it is unofficial and made by Oliver and Patrick Evans.

What evidence it gives

The page offers only its own interface and credits. It gives no data beyond the film count.

Interests and limits

The page carries an Amazon affiliate notice and a donation link. Only the interface text was read, because the film list loads by script. The app was not used.
