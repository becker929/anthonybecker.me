The Three Ways: The Principles Underpinning DevOps

What it is

Gene Kim wrote the post for the IT Revolution site. It presents the Three Ways as values behind the DevOps Handbook and The Phoenix Project. The post sets out to give DevOps a short shared vocabulary.

What it says

  • Kim says the Three Ways are principles from which all DevOps patterns can be derived.
  • The First Way covers the whole system and never passes known defects downstream.
  • The Second Way creates right-to-left feedback loops and aims to shorten and amplify them.
  • The Third Way builds a culture of experimentation, with repetition and practice leading to mastery.
  • Third Way outcomes include time for improving daily work and introducing faults to build resilience.

What evidence it gives

The post offers definitions only. It cites Deming once and gives no case study, data or example. Later patterns are promised for a future post.

Interests and limits

The page is dated 2012 but names later books, so the text changed afterward. Kim co-wrote the books the post names, and IT Revolution publishes them. The page also carries site menus, book adverts and a repeated FAQ block. The post makes no test of whether the Three Ways improve results.
