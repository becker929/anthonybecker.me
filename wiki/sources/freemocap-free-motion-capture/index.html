What it is

The page is written by the FreeMoCap project. It presents the software and links to downloads, code and community chat. It also advertises paid support.

What it says

  • FreeMoCap says it is free, open source, research-grade markerless motion capture software.
  • The page says two or more ordinary webcams, GoPros or phones are enough.
  • Calibration works by waving a printed ChArUco board in view of all cameras.
  • The page says results export as CSV, FBX and .blend files, processed locally.
  • The project says it aims to suit both research scientists and a 13-year-old.
  • The team offers paid professional services for hands-on help.

What evidence it gives

The page offers only the project's own description. It gives no accuracy figures, benchmarks or user studies. Usage counters are shown as placeholders in the fetched text.

Interests and limits

The project promotes its own software and sells services. The video, the images and the live user counters were not viewed. No claim on the page was tested.
