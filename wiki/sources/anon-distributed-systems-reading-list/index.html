What it is

What it says

  • The Amazon section is described as more about culture and organization than technology.
  • The consistency section frames a tradeoff between consistency and availability.
  • The author suggests reading "Paxos Made Simple" before the other Paxos papers and again afterward.

What evidence it gives

The page lists titles with brief annotations and no analysis. It offers no summaries of the papers. Its only support for the choices is the curator's experience.
