What it is

hisamafahri posted a link to Ooh.directory. The site lists blogs by category and is run by philgyford as a hobby. Many comments follow, and the site's maintainer replies often.

What it says

  • Commenter throwaway150 complained that suggested blogs were never added and the review process was opaque.
  • The maintainer, philgyford, said blogs are added when time allows. 2,888 suggestions remain unevaluated.
  • Several commenters said the site's curation is its value, and others called the complaints entitled.
  • simonw suggested human-curated directories may be due a resurgence given worry about AI-generated junk.
  • Commenters offered alternatives: Minifeed, Kagi Small Web, Marginalia, webrings, blogs.hn and other blog lists.
  • Commenters asked for foreign-language blogs and a default sort other than alphabetical. One noted no emacs blogs.

What evidence it gives

The thread gives opinions and personal anecdotes only. The maintainer gives a few facts, such as the suggestion backlog. No usage data on directories is offered.

Interests and limits

Comments are unverified and many are heated. A single complaint dominates the opening. Links to other tools were not opened or checked. The site itself was not reviewed, only what commenters say about it.
