What it is

ElevenLabs publishes this page in its documentation. It explains a feature called Music Finetunes. The page walks through uploading tracks, creating a Finetune and generating music with it.

What it says

  • A Music Finetune is the ElevenLabs music model tuned on the user's uploaded audio.
  • Uploads must not include copyrighted music, even tracks the user bought, licensed or performed. Qualifying Enterprise plans are the exception.
  • A Finetune takes up to 50 tracks, each 10 to 600 seconds long.
  • Creation takes about 5-10 minutes. A Finetune that fails copyright screening is rejected without a refund.
  • The Finetune sets the style. The prompt sets content, mood, tempo, language and structure.
  • The page advises checking that outputs fit the dataset without copying any track.

What evidence it gives

The page offers instructions, limits and advice only. It gives no test results, samples or measures of how closely outputs match training audio.

Interests and limits

ElevenLabs sells this feature, so the page describes it favourably. Claims of stylistic learning rather than reproduction are the vendor's own, untested. A notice about a deprecated music model on the page was not examined. The page itself warns that small or repetitive datasets raise the risk of similar output. Nothing was run or heard.
