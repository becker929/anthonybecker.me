What it is

The thread was posted by alihm. It links to an article on worksinprogress.news. The article itself was not fetched. Its survey result reaches this page only through quotes in the comments.

What it says

  • The opening comment quotes the article's survey. Respondents preferred the traditional design by 74 percent to 26 percent.
  • Many commenters credit cost. They say new buildings are plain because financial considerations come first.
  • Others raise survivorship bias. They say only the best old buildings are still standing.
  • One commenter rejects the cost explanation. They say architects build ugly buildings even when cost is not the limit.
  • Several commenters say the survey judged exteriors only. They add that interiors, services and upkeep matter to the people who live there.
  • Some commenters call the article anti-intellectual. Others say experts have ignored public taste.
  • Long side threads dispute what brutalism means and whether Banksy is modern art.

What evidence it gives

The comments give opinion, personal housing stories and links to buildings. One commenter compares a renovated 1923 building and a new one in Spain by price. Another does a heat calculation for glass walls. Little is checked against data.

Interests and limits

The linked article was not read, only what commenters quote from it. Commenters are anonymous and self-selected. Several posts are heated, and the moderator posts a reminder of the site guidelines. The thread shows what some readers think, not what people in general prefer.
