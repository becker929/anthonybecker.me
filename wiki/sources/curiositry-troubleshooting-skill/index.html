What it is

Curiositry writes the blog The Autodidacts. The essay defines troubleshooting as finding and fixing the cause of unwanted behaviour. It records what the author tries to remember when stuck.

What it says

  • Curiositry says troubleshooting can be honed apart from any domain. It starts with stepping back.
  • The essay advises first doing something certain to have an effect.
  • Curiositry advises tracing flows, then isolating the failing step by testing cut points.
  • The essay says to weigh the stakes, including danger to people, before acting.
  • Curiositry says to gather as much information as possible, ideally while the error occurs.
  • The essay says good troubleshooters understand why a part failed and replace the smallest component.
  • Curiositry says the author's troubleshooting seems better, but hedges and has not tested it.

What evidence it gives

The essay offers anecdotes: a mechanic race, a logging system, a pump rebuild. It gives no measurements. Its proposed experiment was not run.

Interests and limits

This is one person's experience, mostly with cars, electronics and software. The author says outright that the experiment was not done. The essay admits not every problem is a troubleshooting problem. The page includes a paywalled link and a newsletter prompt.
