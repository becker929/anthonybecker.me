What it is

achtaitaipai posted the library to Hacker News. Commenters tried the demos and the editor. The thread covers naming, licensing, teaching use and similar tools.

What it says

  • Commenters praised the example games and the editor's typing and autocomplete.
  • Asked about the word narrative, the creator cited its turn-based dialogue focus but doubted it.
  • Commenters likened it to ZZT, Bitsy, PuzzleScript and RPG Maker.
  • A commenter noted the missing license, and the creator promised an MIT-style one.
  • One commenter called it a good fit for teaching children around age ten.

What evidence it gives

The thread offers opinions, anecdotes and links to example games. It has no benchmarks or tests. The creator's replies explain design choices.

Interests and limits

This is early reaction from self-selected Hacker News readers. The creator answers many comments, which shapes the thread. The library and linked games were not run. The thread is a snapshot from June 2025.
