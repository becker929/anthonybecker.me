Reimagining the mouse pointer for the AI era

What it is

Adrien Baranes and Rob Marchant of Google DeepMind write about a smarter mouse pointer. It would understand what it points at and why. The post states design principles and links to demos in Google AI Studio. It also announces related features in Chrome and a laptop experience.

What it says

  • DeepMind says AI tools in their own window make users drag their work into them.
  • DeepMind lists four principles, including keeping the flow across apps and using "this" and "that".
  • The post says the pointer captures context, so users need not write long prompts.
  • The post says pointing plus speech could carry shorthand like "Fix this".
  • The post says the pointer can turn pixels into places, dates and objects.
  • DeepMind says pointer features are arriving in Gemini in Chrome now and in Googlebook later.

What evidence it gives

The post offers illustrative examples and demo videos, some described as shortened. It links to two experiments in Google AI Studio. One edits an image and one finds places on a map. It gives no user testing, measurements or comparisons.

Interests and limits

Google DeepMind builds and sells the products described. The post is a vision and product announcement, not a study. The demo videos were not viewed, and only the page text was read. Claims about ease and flow are untested in the post. The Googlebook feature is described as coming soon.
