What it is

The thread has 775 points. It discusses a post by Jordan Fulghum. The post describes a small home server, set up with Claude Code. Commenters argue over networking, security and whether this counts as self-hosting.

What it says

  • Jordan Fulghum's linked post says mini PCs, Tailscale and Claude Code make self-hosting fun.
  • Commenter simonw argued Tailscale may matter more than the agent.
  • Commenters split on Tailscale versus plain WireGuard, citing convenience, NAT handling and access rules.
  • Others said a few hardened open ports are fine and Tailscale adds risk.
  • Many commenters objected to using a closed AI service to manage a self-hosted server.
  • Commenters shared both good setup stories and warnings about lost data and weak security.
  • One commenter said the author runs an AI company. Others said it read as AI-written.

What evidence it gives

The post is one person's account of one setup, with a list of services. The thread adds many anecdotes, some of them cautionary. No measurements or tests are offered.

Interests and limits

One commenter said the author runs an AI company, which was not checked. The author replied without denying it. The thread is very long. Only its opening chains of replies were read in full. Later top-level comments were read in shortened form. Most replies to those were skipped.
