What it is

Uri writes the Atoms vs Bits blog. The post sets out 21 numbered tips for throwing parties. It frames hosting as a public service.

What it says

  • Uri says mediocre pizza from a happy host beats fine food from a frazzled one.
  • Uri advises advertising a quarter-to start time, so people arrive near the real hour.
  • Uri says most people attend only if they expect to know 3+ others.
  • Uri advises keeping invite gender ratios no worse than 60-40, to avoid a downward spiral.
  • Uri says in the author's circles 1/3rd of those who say yes do not come.
  • Uri suggests encouraging standing and spreading food and drinks apart to keep guests moving.
  • Uri's last tip calls escalating volume the biggest problem and asks readers for a fix.

What evidence it gives

The post offers experience and rules of thumb only. The flake rate comes from the author's circles. The 70% threshold is a guess marked with a question mark. No data or outside sources are cited.

Interests and limits

The advice reflects one social scene and is not tested. Some claims, like the gender-balance threshold, are hedged with a question mark. The page carries promotions for products by the blog's co-author and friends.
