What it is

The post links to makingsoftware.com. Commenters name the author as Dan Hollick. The book is described as a manual of how things work.

What it says

  • Commenters say the subtitle sits oddly with a description that disclaims teaching making software.
  • Several readers praise the landing page and illustrations. Some ask for a coffee-table book.
  • One commenter criticizes the multi-column layout, justified text and never-ending animations as hard to read.
  • Others defend the animations and suggest a reduced-motion setting as a middle ground.
  • Commenters point out that the page is only an announcement and no chapters are finished.
  • Some report errors, such as a touch-screen line naming a magnetic field.
  • The thread compares the project to The Way Things Work and Engineering in Plain Sight.

What evidence it gives

The thread offers opinion and anecdote only. A few commenters quote the page's own text and FAQ. One physics correction is argued at some length.

Interests and limits

Commenters were reacting to an announcement page, not to a finished book. The thread's own page was read in full, but the linked site was not opened. Opinions on layout and accessibility come from a few individuals. The poster's reason for sharing is not stated.
