What it is

Wikipedia contributors summarize the first book of The Night's Dawn Trilogy. The article covers publication, background, plot, reception and a list of reviews.

What it says

  • The novel opens The Night's Dawn Trilogy and appeared in the UK in 1996.
  • The setting is the 26th and 27th centuries.
  • Humanity has split into Edenists, who share a telepathic gene, and technology-using Adamists.
  • One possessed person says the possessing entities are trapped souls of the dead.
  • A captured possessed says their combined will could move a planet to another plane.
  • Cliff Ramshaw's Arcane review gave the novel 8 out of 10.
  • Ramshaw said the early chapters flounder and the pace picks up after page 300.
