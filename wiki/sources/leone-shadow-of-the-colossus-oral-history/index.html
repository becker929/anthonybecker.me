What it is

Design Room, a games-industry publication, ran this piece for the game's 20th anniversary. Leone interviewed 14 people, 11 of whom worked on the game. The piece arranges their quotes into a narrative.

What it says

  • Director Fumito Ueda says the game began as project Nico, a cooperative online monster-hunting idea.
  • Ueda cut the multiplayer early, judging that the project could not realistically support it.
  • The piece says Ueda has often described 48 planned colossi, cut to 16.
  • Ueda and Kenji Kaido say they showed managers edited footage to extend the schedule.
  • Ueda says a plan for music that changed with the fight was dropped for crossfading.
  • Sony America staff say they asked for save shrines and easier bosses after testing.
  • Kyle Shubel recalls about 800,000 units sold in America, and over a million worldwide.

What evidence it gives

The piece rests entirely on interview recollections from participants. Some quotes were translated from Japanese. Accounts sometimes conflict, for example over whether the US office turned the game down. Sales figures are from memory.

Interests and limits

Interviewees recall events from 20 years ago and may flatter themselves or their employer. Several worked for Sony, which published the game. Extended interviews are paywalled and were not part of the text read. Nearly all voices are Sony staff, with a composer and one outside programmer.
