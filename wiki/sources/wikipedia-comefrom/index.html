What it is

Wikipedia editors describe a statement that makes control jump away from a named point. The article covers history, semantics and four examples.

What it says

  • COMEFROM sends control to the statement after it when a named point is reached.
  • Goto depends on local code structure, while COMEFROM depends on global structure.
  • The interpreter must scan for COMEFROM statements. Nothing near a jump point shows a jump.
  • Semantics vary by language, including whether the jump happens before or after the target statement.
  • A 1973 Datamation article by R. Lawrence Clark answered Dijkstra's goto letter, and C-INTERCAL later implemented COMEFROM.
  • Richie Hindle published a working Python COMEFROM on 1 April 2004 using debugger hooks.
  • In OS/360 Fortran G, the debug AT statement acts like COMEFROM.

What evidence it gives

It cites a Datamation article, a SIGPLAN Notices paper, an IBM manual and a newsletter. It gives code in a hypothetical BASIC, Python, Ruby and OS/360 Fortran G.

Interests and limits

Wikipedia is edited by volunteers and one claim is marked citation needed. The examples were not run. The article treats COMEFROM mainly as humor.
