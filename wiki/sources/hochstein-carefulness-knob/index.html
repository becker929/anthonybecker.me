What it is

Lorin Hochstein writes about software, complex systems and incidents. The post uses a play to question calls for teams to be more careful.

What it says

  • In the play, a tech lead says delivery depends on a carefulness knob.
  • The tech lead draws an exponential curve of time against carefulness.
  • The tech lead says being less careful would not speed delivery.
  • The tech lead says risk judgments are good but imperfect, so incidents still happen.
  • The play ends with another manager demanding more care after an incident.
  • The coda names the trade-off as the efficiency-thoroughness trade-off, or ETTO principle.
  • The coda claims the trade-off is made implicitly, since nobody wants a long estimate.

What evidence it gives

The post offers argument through a fictional scene and a hand-drawn curve in the story. It cites no data, studies or real incidents.

Interests and limits

The exponential curve is an illustration inside the story and was not measured. The post is one author's view of how teams behave. Two reader comments disagree about its point. The post names no tested remedy.
