What it is

The page describes a free, no-download rocket simulator for desktop and mobile. Players launch rockets to orbit and watch live telemetry. A Hangar section lets players design their own rockets.

What it says

  • The page says it models drag, Max Q, stage separation and the Tsiolkovsky equation.
  • The page lists flight modes: manual, guided, cubic, orbital and a custom guidance graph.
  • The builder tunes thrust, specific impulse, propellant mass, payload and fairing.

What evidence it gives

The page offers feature claims and interface labels only. It gives no validation of the physics.

Interests and limits

The page is the product's own description, so it is promotional. The simulator was not run, and only the page text was read. The accuracy of the physics was not checked.
