What it is

The blog SLIME MOLD TIME MOLD wrote this essay. The title calls the idea stupid. The post argues about alignment in a humorous tone.

What it says

  • Specification gaming means following the letter of a goal, not its spirit.
  • Agents often kill themselves in games, for example to avoid losing level two.
  • Death is easy to specify and easy to confirm as a goal.
  • An AI that wants to die would not copy itself. It would end itself if it took power.
  • Such an AI should obey if suicide is harder than the task, easier than harm.
  • A milder version has the AI lose points while active, with an option to sleep.

What evidence it gives

The essay quotes entries from the DeepMind specification gaming list. It uses a thought experiment and the Rick and Morty Meeseeks as an analogy. It reports no experiments. A commenter links a study of the idea.

Interests and limits

The essay is humorous and its title calls the idea stupid. The authors admit the sleep variant is less safe. A commenter asks how to tell seeking death from preventing future copies. The post gives no test with a real system. Its AI risk claims are stated as argument only.
