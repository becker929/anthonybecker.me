What it is

Michelle Brush, an Engineering Director at Google, gave the talk at SREcon26 Americas in Seattle. The page lists the 45-minute slot and a short abstract. Brush leads the SRE teams for Compute Engine and Persistent Disk.

What it says

  • The abstract says AI-written code will make systems almost nondeterministic, beyond old methods.
  • The abstract lists four strategies: rigorous evaluations, generic mitigations, recreating reproducibility, and risk-first development.
  • The abstract says humans will always be critical to taming complexity.
