Glossary

300dpi
A print-quality image resolution of 300 dots per inch.
Ankara
A colourful printed wax cloth common in West Africa.
Glazing
Layering thin transparent paint over dry paint.
Impasto
A painting style that applies thick, opaque paint.
Kamen Rider
A Japanese live-action superhero franchise.
Lospec
A website for pixel art resources, including a jobs listing.
MORPHO
A series of figure-drawing reference books, shown here as four titled volumes.
Natural hair movement
A movement that encourages Black people to wear hair in its natural texture.
Node-based
A design where behaviour is built by linking boxes that each do one task.
OC
An original character, made by the client or artist.
Pin
A saved image or link on a Pinterest board.
Pixel art
Digital art drawn at low resolution, one square pixel at a time.
Reference book
A book of images used for studying from, rather than for reading.
Soft proofing
Previewing on screen how colours will look when printed.
Spectral reflectance
How much light of each wavelength a surface reflects.
Venice Biennale
A large international art exhibition held in Venice.
VGen
An online marketplace where artists list commissions and clients order them.
