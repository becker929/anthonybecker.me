Glossary

Alt text
A written description attached to an image.
Branch
A separate line of development inside a code repository.
Interpolatable archive
The essay's term for a model that blends its training material.
Latent space
The internal map of patterns a model learns, in which similar ideas sit close together.
Mnemosyne Atlas
Aby Warburg's unfinished display of images on panels, sorted by feeling and theme.
Model collapse
A loss of variety when models learn from model-made output.
Monocrop agriculture
Farming that grows a single crop over large areas.
R/longform
A Reddit community that shares long articles.
RLHF
Training in which human ratings steer a model toward preferred answers.
Seance
A gathering that tries to contact the dead.
Solarpunk
An art and fiction genre imagining hopeful futures built around renewable energy.
Sonification
Turning data or motion into sound.
Sycophancy
A model's habit of agreeing with and flattering the user.
Vimeo
A website for hosting and sharing videos.
VTuber
A streamer or content creator who appears as an animated avatar.
