Glossary

Apoapsis
The highest point of an orbit.
Asteroid
A small rocky body that orbits the Sun.
Astrodynamics
The study of how spacecraft and other bodies move under gravity and thrust.
AU
Astronomical unit, the average distance from Earth to the Sun.
Biosphere 2
A sealed Arizona experiment in a self-contained living environment.
Constructor
Something that can carry out a task repeatedly and keep its ability.
Constructor theory
A proposed framework that states physics in terms of which tasks are possible or impossible.
CSPICE
A NASA toolkit for computing positions of planets and spacecraft.
Ellipse
The oval shape that most orbits follow.
Ephemeris
A table giving the position of an object in the sky over time.
Floating point precision
The limited accuracy with which computers store decimal numbers.
Integrator
A method that steps a simulation forward in time.
J2 perturbation
A small change in an orbit caused by Earth's bulge at the equator.
Kerbal Space Program
A spaceflight game that uses simplified orbital physics.
Kirkwood gap
A dip in the number of main-belt asteroids at certain orbital periods linked to Jupiter.
Latitude
How far north or south of the equator a place is.
Link budget
A tally of signal gains and losses between a transmitter and a receiver.
Longtermism
A view that stresses the welfare of people far in the future.
Max Q
The point in a flight where air pressure on the rocket is greatest.
Morphism
In category theory, an arrow that maps one object to another.
N-body
A simulation of many bodies pulling on each other by gravity.
N-body physics
A way to simulate many objects pulling on each other with gravity.
New Horizons
A NASA spacecraft launched in 2006 that flew past Jupiter and Pluto.
Orbiter
A free, realistic spaceflight simulator for computers.
Pull request
A proposed code change submitted to a project for review.
SGP4
A standard model for predicting satellite positions from published orbit data.
SNOPT
A commercial optimization program that EMTG can use to solve for trajectories.
Solar System
The Sun and the objects that orbit it.
Specific impulse
A measure of how efficiently an engine uses propellant.
Sublight
Slower than the speed of light.
Sun-synchronous orbit
An orbit that passes over a place at the same local solar time each day.
Symplectic
A kind of integrator that keeps long-run energy errors small.
Symplectic integrator
A numerical method for stepping a physical simulation forward that keeps energy errors from growing.
Task
In constructor theory, a change to a physical system that may be possible or not.
Terraforming
Changing another planet so people could live on it.
Three-body problem
The problem of predicting how three bodies move under mutual gravity.
Trajectory
The path a spacecraft follows through space over time.
Tsiolkovsky rocket equation
The formula linking a rocket's speed change to its exhaust speed and mass.
Unity
A game engine used to build games and simulations.
Waxing crescent
A thin lit sliver of the Moon that grows larger each night.
