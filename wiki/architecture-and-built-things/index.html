Glossary

Brutalism
A building style that leaves concrete raw and exposed.
Dymaxion
Fuller's name for several inventions, including a house, a car and a map.
Flight controller
The small computer that keeps a drone stable and carries out movement commands.
Geodesic dome
A light, round building made of a lattice of triangles.
Massing
Designing a building first as a composition of large volumes.
NIMBY
A resident who opposes new building near their home.
Raspberry Pi
A small, low-cost computer often used in hobby projects.
Survivorship bias
Judging the past only by the few things that lasted.
Synergetics
Fuller's own system of geometry and thinking about structure.
Tensegrity
A structure held up by balanced tension and compression members.
Time-of-flight camera
A camera that measures distance by timing how long emitted light takes to return.
