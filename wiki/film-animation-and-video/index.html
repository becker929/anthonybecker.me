Glossary

Android
A robot built to look and act like a human.
B-movie
A low-budget commercial film, often made as a second feature.
Bug ferrets
An alien crew species named in the page text.
Character actor
An actor known for playing distinctive supporting roles rather than conventional leads.
ChArUco board
A printed checkerboard with coded markers, used to calibrate cameras.
Cytherean
Of Venus; the word comes from the planet's old poetic name.
Fallout
A series of video games set after a nuclear war.
FBX
A common file format for 3D models and animation.
FFmpeg
A widely used open-source program for converting and editing video.
IndexedDB
A database built into web browsers for storing data on the user's own device.
Letterboxd
A social site where people log, rate and review films.
Markerless motion capture
Recording body movement in 3D from video, without suits or reflective dots.
Metal Heroes
A Japanese franchise of armored hero television series.
Remotion
A library for making videos from React code.
Sentai
A Japanese genre of colour-coded hero teams.
Show HN
A label on Hacker News for posts where someone shows a project they made.
Spine number
The number Criterion gives each release in its collection.
Supplement
An extra section of a site, here one collecting old comics.
Teddy Award
A prize given at the Berlin film festival for films with queer themes.
TMDB
The Movie Database, a community-edited source of film information.
Tokusatsu
Japanese live-action fiction with special effects, often about costumed heroes.
Torchship
A spacecraft that burns fuel continuously for fast travel.
VFX
Short for visual effects, the work of creating or altering images for film.
Voice actor
A performer who provides the voice for animated or game characters.
WebAssembly
A format that lets compiled programs run inside a web browser.
Wikidata
A free, shared database of facts maintained by volunteers.
