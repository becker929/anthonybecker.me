Research · listening test

Help us test sound by ear

About 5 minutes for every 10 pairs.

This is a short listening test. You will hear pairs of sounds.

Each pair is the same sound with one thing changed. We ask what job each sound has. Then we ask which one fits a job better.

There are 40 pairs. Each pair takes about 30 seconds.

Your answers are anonymous. We do not store your name, email, or IP address. Answers save as you go, so even a short visit still helps. You can stop at any time.

What the jobs mean

  • kick — the beat hit, once every count.
  • rumble — a low hum that fills gaps.
  • hat — a short tick sound.
  • clap — a snare-like hit, on some beats.
  • hook — the catchy part you remember.
  • space — a pad or rise that marks change.
