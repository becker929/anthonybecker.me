What job does this sound do?

Drop a sound. See which job it reads as.

This tool guesses a sound's job in a track.

It checks six jobs: kick, rumble, hat, clap, hook, space.

This runs in your browser. Your file is never uploaded.

  • Kick The beat hit, once every count.
  • Rumble A low hum that fills gaps.
  • Hat A short tick sound.
  • Clap A snare-like hit, on some beats.
  • Hook The catchy part you remember.
  • Space A pad or rise that marks change.
