Counting the video games of 2024

11 October 2026 · written by Claude (Opus 5.5) · plain language · glossary

What we asked, and what we found

How many video games came out in 2024? How sure can anyone be of that number? And which of those games did critics and juries single out? This project answered all three from public data.

We counted 18,033 games first released in 2024. Allowing for games that every list missed, we estimate 18,400 to 20,400 in all. Almost all are PC games. Steam carries 97% of the year's games. Nearly half got fewer than ten player reviews. Critics scored only 394 of them.

Acclaim begins at a Metascore of 85. Seventy games of 2024 reach it. The same method put the line at 85 in 2023 and 2025 too. Typical scores keep rising, though. Across many years, the line works better as a share of each year's scored games.

How many games came out

First we had to say what one game is. One game is one record, however many platforms it reached. A game belongs to the year of its first release on an in-scope platform. That means PC, home consoles, handhelds with swappable game media, and arcade machines. Mobile-only, browser-only and standalone VR games are out. Demos, add-ons and soundtracks are out too.

A game in early access counts from its early-access launch. So 2024 has two honest totals. By first release, it has 18,033 games. By the date of the finished version, it has 16,467. In all, 965 games began early access during 2024.

Every list of games misses some. So we built the count from five sources and compared their overlaps. That method is called capture-recapture. A figure read straight from the records is counted. A figure from a model is estimated, and always comes as a range.

PC games are nearly all found. Steam alone holds 17,525 of the counted games. Two curated lists suggest that 594 to 908 PC games of 2024 skipped Steam. We saw 247 of them through other sources. Games sold only on Epic's store, Microsoft's store or publishers' launchers fit that gap.

Console-only games are the weak spot. No catalog we could use without a key lists them in full. We counted 261, but the estimate runs from 674 to 1,584.

The count still holds some known errors. About 68 records are likely duplicates. About 1.9% may be older games re-released. Net of both, the count would be near 17,618, within 16,067 to 17,943.

The 2024 count is 30.0% above 2023's, measured the same way. 2023 had 13,874 games. Our provisional count for 2025 is 20,901, a rise of 15.9%. SteamDB's December 2025 snapshot shows a similar 31.5% rise for 2024.

Releases grew through the year. January held 6.6% of the year's games. October was busiest, with 1,847 games. The last three months held 28.9%.

Most games were cheap. Of all counted games, 75.8% are free or under $10 on Steam. Only 288 list at $40 or more.

Most games found few players

Player reviews on Steam give a rough sense of who played what. They are very unequal. The median 2024 game on Steam has 12 reviews. Of the 17,525 games on Steam, 8,043 have fewer than ten. That is 44.6% of the whole year's count.

We use fewer than ten reviews as a shovelware flag. It marks games that almost nobody bought or reviewed. It says nothing about their quality. Leave flagged games out, and the 2024 count falls to 9,990. The rise over 2023 falls from 30.0% to 21.1%.

Attention gathers at the very top. The top 1% of games hold 76.0% of all reviews. Black Myth: Wukong leads with 882,308 reviews. Helldivers 2 follows with 841,242. Those counts were read in October 2026.

Valve began asking developers to disclose AI-generated content in January 2024. We read 709 store pages, a random 4% sample. Of the 687 pages in the 2024 count, 76 carry the disclosure. That is 11.1%, with a 95% interval of 8.9% to 13.6%. It implies about 1,600 to 2,400 games. The count includes disclosures added after launch, so it is not the share at release.

What critics scored

A Metascore is Metacritic's weighted average of critic scores. A game needs at least four critic reviews to get one. Only 394 games of our 2024 count have a Metascore. That is 2.18% of the year.

Metacritic files 455 games under 2024. Our rules move some of them to other years and drop a few add-ons. Some began early access before 2024, and some came out earlier in Japan. That leaves 377 in our count. Another 17 that Metacritic files under other years join them, making 394.

We use each game's best score across platforms. Scores bunch in the high 70s. The median is 78. The middle half runs from 72 to 82. Only 15 games reached 90.

Hades II scored highest, at 98 on Switch. It counts as a 2024 game because its early access began in May 2024. Balatro scored 95 on Switch. Astro Bot, Metaphor: ReFantazio and Braid, Anniversary Edition each scored 94.

Where acclaim begins

An acclaim gate is a score line that marks a game as acclaimed. To place it fairly, we needed a judge that uses no critic scores. Otherwise the test would grade itself.

That judge is recognition. A game is recognised if two independent outlets listed it among the year's best. A nomination at a major award show also counts. So does a Golden Joystick win.

In 2024, 78 games are recognised, and 70 of them have a Metascore. We then found the lowest score whose gate holds no more than those 70. This is the count-matching score. For 2024 it is 85. Resampling the scored games 2,000 times puts it between 84 and 86.

The gate admits 70 games of 2024. That is 17.8% of scored games, and 0.39% of all games. Juries alone would put the gate at 86. Best-of lists alone would put it at 89.

The same method lands on 85 in 2023, 2024 and 2025. A second method fits a curve to find where recognition becomes more likely than not. That score, S*, ranges from 87.5 to 88.8. A third takes each year's top tenth, whose cut runs from 85 to 87. Only the count-matching score holds still.

Critics and juries agree at the very top, then part ways. Of the 70 games at 85 or more, juries or lists recognised 33. Black Myth: Wukong shows the split. It won the Golden Joystick for game of the year. Yet its best Metascore sits below 85.

A wider union gate admits a game on its score or on recognition. It holds 115 games in 2024. It is the steadiest count, at 115 to 117 a year from 2023 to 2025.

Astro Bot swept the main prizes for 2024 games. It won Game of the Year at The Game Awards and at DICE. It also won BAFTA's Best Game. Balatro won the Game Developers Choice Award for Game of the Year.

The longer view

This project also checks an earlier one, a ladder of acclaimed games across all years. Each rung holds about 3.16 times as many games as the one below. Rung 7 means about 1,000 games, and rung 8 about 3,000. The ladder guessed that acclaim ends near rung 8.

The ladder's table counts the games critics scored in each year. From 2002 to 2024, that number ran between 450 and 632. So Metacritic's 455 games for 2024 sit within the usual range, near its low end. Our recheck of the source file found the same 455. Releases jumped 30% in 2024, yet the critics' count did not rise.

Typical scores have crept up. Metacritic's median was 68 in 2007 and 79 in 2025. From 2015 to 2021, 9.4% to 11.5% of scored games reached 85. In Metacritic's own 2024 list, 80 of 455 games did, or 17.6%. A fixed score of 85 lets in a growing share as scores rise. So across many years, the gate works better as a share.

As a share, the gate is the top 17.6% of each year's scored games. Across 1989 to 2025, that gives about 2,577 games, on rung 7.8. The union gate gives about 4,278, on rung 8.3. That sits inside the ladder's earlier guess of 4,000 to 5,000.

How it was done

Five sources

Steam is Valve's PC game store. We asked its public API about every app number, from 10 to 5.6 million. That gave an almost complete list of PC games on Steam. Wikidata added dates, platforms and links between catalogs. Wikipedia added its list of 2024 releases and its console game lists. Metacritic scores came from a public dataset, copied from Metacritic's site in October 2025.

Merging records into games

The sources had to be merged into one record per game. This step is called entity resolution. Shared IDs come first, such as a Steam number stored in Wikidata. Exact titles with close dates come next. Close but inexact title matches come last, under strict rules. Titles whose numbers differ never merge, so sequels stay apart.

A helper agent checked 200 merges by hand. Three were wrong, a rate of 1.5%. Two joined numbered sequels, and one joined two games that share a title. The rules were tightened after the check. A fresh audit of the final merges is still to do.

Estimating what every source missed

Capture-recapture treats each source as a net cast over the same games. If two nets catch many of the same games, few escaped both. If they rarely overlap, many escaped.

Our nets lean on each other. Wikidata copied thousands of Steam games, so that pair says little. Notable games land in every net, while small ones land in one or none.

So we used methods built for that. The Chapman estimator works on two lists. Log-linear models handle several lists. Chao's bound sets a floor on the total.

For PC, we paired Steam with Wikipedia's 2024 list, which was built apart from Steam. That pair estimates 18,433 PC games, within 18,150 to 18,852. The best log-linear model gives 18,356, within 18,003 to 18,777. The PC range keeps our observed 17,772 as its low end.

Console-only games are harder. Of 259 in the model, 189 appear in only one source. Just 43 appear in exactly two, a sign that many more were missed. Chao's bound turns those numbers into a floor of 674. The top, 1,584, comes from a less stable model. It sits well inside console store counts.

Checks against outside counts

SteamDB counted 18,558 Steam releases in 2024, in a December 2025 snapshot. We found 17,525 games of 2024 on Steam. The gap has a known cause. We date early-access games and ports by their first launch. For example, 716 games left early access in 2024 after starting earlier.

Access

Our own pulls ran on 11 October 2026, one request at a time. We used three official interfaces: Steam's Web API, the Wikipedia API and Wikidata's query service. Each was used within its published limits. The Steam scan made about 2,240 calls, far below Valve's cap of 100,000 a day. It slowed down whenever Steam asked it to.

The robots.txt files on those three services bar all automated agents. We read that rule as aimed at web crawlers. Each service publishes separate rules for programs, which we followed. Other sites whose robots.txt blocks AI agents were not fetched. Metacritic itself was not fetched either. No API keys, accounts or paid data were used.

Limits and open questions

  • Console-only games are seen only through Wikipedia, Wikidata and Metacritic. Their range is wide.
  • Steam lists only PC platforms. So our console counts by family are floors. We count 707 PlayStation, 696 Nintendo and 498 Xbox games. GameDiscoverCo counted about 2,900 new Switch games in 2024, ports included.
  • The shovelware flag measures attention on Steam. Games without a Steam page cannot be flagged. Games of 2025 had less time to gather reviews.
  • The Metacritic dataset stops on 7 October 2025. It holds no review counts.
  • The panel of best-of lists is small, with ten outlets for 2024. Sixteen of 28 candidate outlets block AI agents, or hide behind a bot wall.
  • The merge audit came before the final fixes. A second audit would confirm the final error rate.
  • Some duplicate records remain among console-only games. Removing them would lower the console floor from 674 to about 470.
  • Three more catalogs need keys or payment: IGDB, MobyGames and OpenCritic. IGDB would most improve the console estimate.
  • Is fewer than ten reviews the right shovelware test? A rule based on price or store tags could replace it.
  • Should the ladder use one fixed score, or a share of each year? The project's default is the share. Anthony has not yet confirmed it.

Who did this work

Claude agents did this work under Anthony Becker's direction. A lead agent wrote the pipeline and the reports. Helper agents gathered awards, best-of lists and outside facts. A separate verifier agent, which had not seen the work, then checked it.

The verifier rebuilt every output from the raw pulls and got identical files. It fetched a sample of Steam records again. Names, dates and platforms all matched. It checked 30 records against their sources, and 28 agreed. It recomputed 102 figures in code, and 100 matched.

It also found demos and free prologues inside the count. The lead removed them and made other fixes. Together, these lowered the count from 18,241 to 18,033. The final numbers have not had a second full check. Another verifier agent checked this page against the project's files.

Glossary

acclaim gate
A score line that marks a game as acclaimed. Here it is a best Metascore of 85.
API
A documented way for programs to ask a service for data.
capture-recapture
Estimating a total from how much several lists overlap.
Chao's bound
A floor on a total when some items are far easier to catch than others.
Chapman estimator
A two-list estimate of a total, from the size of each list and their overlap.
count-matching score
The lowest score whose gate holds no more games than the scored, recognised games.
counted
A figure read straight from the records, with no model.
early access
A paid release of an unfinished game. The finished version is called 1.0.
entity resolution
Deciding which records from different sources describe the same game.
estimated
A figure produced by a model. It is always given as a range.
GameRankings
A site that averaged critic scores. Our figures before 2000 come from a 2010 copy of its data.
in scope
Counted by this project. PC, consoles, handhelds with swappable media and arcade are in scope.
95% interval
A range built so that it holds the true value in 95 of 100 repeats.
log-linear model
A model of which sources list each game. It lets pairs of sources lean on each other.
median
The middle value. Half are above it, and half below.
Metascore
Metacritic's weighted average of critic scores, out of 100. It needs at least four reviews.
recognition
Two independent best-of lists, a major award nomination, or a Golden Joystick win.
robots.txt
A file on a website that tells automated programs which pages they may read.
rung
A step on the acclaimed games ladder. Each holds about 3.16 times the one below.
S*
The score at which a game becomes more likely than not to be recognised.
shovelware flag
Our marker for a Steam game with fewer than ten user reviews. It measures attention only.
Steam
Valve's online store for PC games. Each game has a store page with player reviews.
SteamDB
An independent tracker of Steam's catalog.
union gate
A gate that admits a game scoring 85 or more, or one with recognition.
Wikidata
An open database of facts that anyone may reuse, released under CC0.
